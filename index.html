Plan for most of the day.
Your evaluation includes a comprehensive developmental battery — testing typically runs 3–4 hours after the clinical interview. Tests are non-invasive and completed live over Zoom using oral questions, written tasks, and standardized procedures. Take breaks when you need them and go at your own pace.
Bringing someone with you?
A family member or companion is welcome during the interview portion — the neuropsychologist may ask them questions too. Once testing begins, companions are typically asked to step away.
